# Taste profile: what the Commander likes (every agent reads this before every task)

Follow it. Rules are the Commander's own words. "Learned from votes" is distilled from their 👍 / 👎 by the Analyst.

## Commander's rules
- 2026-11-11: Never repeat one of our own past videos or a popular niche video (no Sun-vanishing remakes).
- 2026-11-11: Use several locations (city, town, bay, sky, orbit), not one set. Full-colour people and places, not silhouettes.
- 2026-11-11: I want chaos and drama: something crazy happening on screen, big visible disasters and their aftermath (tsunamis, impacts, things flying into space). "Interesting to look at" is not enough.

## Learned from votes
- Likes: escalation in steps (5 s → 1 min → 10 min), each step bigger and in a new place (city → bay → sky → orbit).
- Likes: aftermath disasters people can see (tsunamis, crashes, meteor storms) and absurd spectacle (planes rising past the space station).
- Dislikes: quiet or subtle premises where nothing violent happens on screen (ice, sound, see-through Earth).
- Dislikes: anything that repeats our own videos or a niche hit.
