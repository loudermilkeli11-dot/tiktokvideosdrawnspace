## What all of them share (observed, not judged)
1. **Premise:** "What if [a basic thing] disappeared or vanished (for 5 seconds)?"
2. **Opening:** a countdown to the event is on screen from frame 1, often with milliseconds.
3. **Type:** a serif title in sentence case with one coloured key word. The counter is a three-part block (label / number / fact) at mid-left, not at the top.
4. **Look:** bright, clean, stylised daylight 3D. Everyday places (park, beach, lake town), with simple people and animals.
5. **The event:** it changes the whole frame at once (colour inversion, night), and new counters then track the aftermath.
6. **Sound:** at least one uses narration with subtitles. Background music and sound effects can't be seen in stills.
