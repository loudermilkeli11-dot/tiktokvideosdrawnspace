STRATEGY: Studio v2 is a 34 s, parametric, counter-driven episode format. It has a bright but cinematic grade, a key-word title, a mid-left counter ticking from frame 1, captions of 6 words or fewer, tick-and-hit sound, no voiceover (VO lines kept optional), and a build that goes from a config to the final file. The Marshal's video #2 is "What if ice sank?"
  why-it-wins:
  - Every officer converges on the format: a countdown to an event, from frame 1, in a bright readable world, at 34 s.
  - That attacks our measured weak point (retention: 26.5% and 8.13% for A119; 20.1% and 2.80% for Pickaxe) with the niche's own grammar, without cloning anyone's signature.

STUDIO v2 FORMAT SPEC (as advised by the Marshal):
- **Look:**
  - A lifted, saturated, readable first frame in daylight. Dusk is a reveal, not the opener.
  - One real-textured hero object.
  - 2 samples by default (measured 6 s per frame on the bay).
- **Type:**
  - Title: sentence case, one key word in the accent colour, at about 24% of the height. It fades after 2.5 s.
  - Counter: three parts (label, a tabular number with milliseconds, a fact line), left-aligned at y about 44%.
  - Captions: 6 words or fewer at y 560–620, with a soft shadow and no box.
  - Small DRAMATIZATION and AI tags at the top left.
  - Serif (A) or grotesque (B) is the Commander's call; the sheet shows both.
- **Sound:**
  - A sub-bass pulse from frame 1, a tick per counter second, a riser into the event, and a hard hit on the event.
  - Mid-range hits on caption changes.
  - Silence as the last beat.
- **Structure:**
  - 0–3 s: the counter is live, the camera moving, a small change in the world.
  - About 8 s: the event, on a hit.
  - Then 4–5 beats of 4–5 s each, each with a new counter label.
  - Last 3 s: the reveal, then a hard loop to frame 1.

BASELINE:
  - Ice sinking is a science-class premise, not an everyday "what if X disappeared" hook. A slow, days-long time-lapse with a bottom-up fill has no instant event that changes the whole frame, so it won't stop the scroll or hold viewers.
  - About 20 hours of infrastructure before shipping anything is over-engineering for a channel with 7K views at best and 0 shares. It delays the learning that only comes from posting.
  - Changing length, look, type, sound and premise all at once means a weak result can't be traced to a cause. Skipping narration also drops the one element a niche hit used.
