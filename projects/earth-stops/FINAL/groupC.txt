RULES I BENT (and why)
- s06 looks NORTH-EAST (32-50 deg), not roughly north. From any south terrace a 105-125 m heap hides the east-shore town if you look
  north; only the south-east town is visible beside the heap. East still reads as screen-right (and away).
- The lighthouse (245, -1690) is NOT in s06: it sits at bearing ~3 deg, outside a north-east frame. It is in s05 (far, at the mouth).
- The pier is not carried: it stands over the drained floor at the bottom-left edge (mostly below frame). Stranded boats carry the
  "it drained" proof instead.
- The brief named bayWorld(S, { slab: true }); I didn't use slab or kit.bayWorld (see FILES for why; same look values).

KNOWN WEAK SPOTS
- The sky is pale beige-grey (kit SKY + HAZE, same as the plaza shots). The reference hits have more golden saturation.
- Puddles are built from ocean vertices, so their edges are blocky up close (they're fine at s05's distance).
- s06 late: the wall's trailing edge has passed the south-shore houses, so their wreckage lies on dry ground in front of the wall.
  That is correct for the law (the water moves on), but a viewer could read it as "knocked down by nothing". It's a small part of the frame.
- Spray streaks in s06 look a little vertical (rain-like) at half size.
- The heap's over-land face is in the sun's shadow (sun from the west), so the leading face is grey-blue rather than bright white.
- Type zones: in s05 the counter sits over the drained floor (calm mid-tone) and the caption band over the town edge (moderately
  busy). In s06 the counter sits over the wall body and the caption over the town. Check contrast with check.py --contrast.
