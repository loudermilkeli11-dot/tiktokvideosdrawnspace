== s01_crossing (0.0-4.0, tau 0->4) ==
What it shows: frame 1 is already moving. The red car (50 km/h, brakeCarX) is 7.6 m short of a new white stop line. Its enlarged brake lights glow
  and throw a red light on the road. 4 m of dark skid marks lie behind it, laid from tau -0.3 to 0. Above the lanes, a NEW signal on a mast arm
  shows red. The car does not slow: it crosses the stop line at about 0.55 s and the zebra at about 0.7-1.0 s, and is through the junction by about 2 s.
  Ahead in the same lane, a blue car (also braking, coasting at 45 km/h) does the same. A yellow taxi caught mid-left-turn (R 16 m, 8 m/s) keeps
  its velocity and its yaw rate (kit spinCar): it glides dead straight and pirouettes off toward the plaza. Three foreground walkers slip at
  0.30 / 0.85 / 1.20 s, fall on their backs and keep gliding away from the lens. The far crowd also slips (walkers fall at 0.15-1.2 s; about 35% of the
  people standing still fall at 0.9-3.5 s). A cafe terrace (9 tables, 2 planters, 1 bin) drifts and turns slowly (<= 0.25 m/s).
  The hero (red coat, tote) stands on the kerb with a small sway: about 76 design px tall at design (321, 398-475), right of the counter block.
  The last 0.35 s whips LEFT: one move across the cut, yaw = whip(film t, 4.0, 0.7, 70), so 0 -> 35 deg in s01 and 35 -> 70 deg in s02.
Best frames: out/frames_s01/s01_crossing_t0.00.png (frame 1) and t1.50.png (the car in the junction under the red light).
Layout: the title zone (y 140-270) shows buildings and sky. The counter block (x 50-290, y 370-500) shows the pavement, a planter and the
  signal pole. The signal heads sit at y ~265-300, between the title and the counter. The caption band shows tables and fallen walkers (calm by 2.9 s).
Sound: a 1.75-2.6 kHz squeal at full level on frame 1, cut dead at 0.25 s; then a pink hiss (high-cut 3 kHz), room tone,
  a rising crowd, a gasp, 6 brown-noise thumps (< 260 Hz) timed to the falls, a rising drone, and a low whoosh (120 -> 900 Hz) on the whip.
Weak spots:
  - The deviation in the next block applies here: the camera is not the kit's FRAME1_CAM.
  - 35 mm with the hero at 20 m keeps the red car mid-distance (about 55 design px wide at frame 1). The hook relies on brake glow + red light + motion.
  - The blue car 2 and the taxi start near the counter block's right edge (x ~230-290, y ~350-380) for the first ~0.5 s.
  - With 12 samples, the whip's sub-sample ghosts are visible in a still frame (about 9 px steps at full size); they are fine in motion.
  - The s01 last frame is mostly the shadowed facade (dark navy); s02's first frame is brighter (pavement, zebra, tree).
    This is a possible upward luma step at the 4.0 cut: please check it with check.py.

== s02_grip (4.0-8.5, tau 4->8.5) ==
What it shows: the whip lands from the right (-35 -> 0 deg in 0.35 s). Then 50 mm from the flat quay road (camera (60, 3.0, 301 -> 299.5),
  slow push), with the zebra in the foreground. 13 cars, a white bus and 17 pavement props slide down at slopeX.
  At tau 5 (shot 1.0 s = film 5.0) they switch to slideSkidX: locked cars skid and stop 1.68 s later.
  Four sideways cars trip and roll about their downhill lower edge (90-180 deg over 0.8-1.2 s; they roll edge over edge, so the centre advances W/2+H/2 per 90 deg).
  The bus, slewed across the right lanes, tips onto its side (90 deg over 1.15 s). Tables cartwheel, bins tip and roll, benches tip.
  Dark rubber skid marks are laid from tau 5 (mostly hidden behind the cars from this angle). Amber hazard lights blink at 1.4 Hz from tau 5.5.
  Kick shake on the hit. No people on the hill.
  The nearest car starts 36 m above the street's foot (the brief said >= 60 m) and stops 8 m above it. That fills the lower frame; nothing reaches the quay road.
Last frame (match cut to s03): street centre line vertical through x 270, gaze +6 deg, vanishing point at design y ~400. The cars are stopped and wrecked.
Best frames: out/frames_s02/s02_grip_t4.30.png (aftermath) and t1.20.png (the trip just starting). Gate F2 frames: t0.50 / t1.20 / t2.50.
Sound: whoosh tail, then the glide (hiss + drone).
  At 1.0: a low crunch (brown noise lowpass 700 Hz) + metal crumple (350-800 Hz bursts) + sub; the score adds the big hit.
  Then 4 skid squeals (1.25-1.9 kHz) that die out by 2.68 s, roll crunches, the bus slam at 2.23 s, low wood/metal knocks,
  two far car alarms (triangle 520/610 Hz, lowpass 900 Hz, through the reverb), wind and a far crowd. No glass tinkles (they would be high tones).
Weak spots:
  - The HILL READ IS WEAK from this handshake framing. Looking straight up a 10 deg street, the slope is a pitch, and from here pitch is invisible:
    only perspective cues remain (the street vanishes 220 px above the true horizon; vertical buildings; the zebra on the flat).
    Gate F2 may fail on the hill read alone; the crash itself reads.
    A stronger fix needs a different angle (side-on or oblique) for tt 0-2.5, then a move back to the on-axis match framing.
  - The left lanes sit behind the counter block, so I kept mid-distance left-lane sliders out. The near blue car and the upturned purple car still overlap the counter's lower edge after 2.5 s (stopped, calm).
  - The kit's box cars (black glass cabins) read as blocks at 20 m; the upturned cars read, the bus on its side reads less.

Kit/law needs (for the lead): none required. Optional: adopt A_CAM as FRAME1_CAM in the kit so the other pages and the cover use the same frame 1.
