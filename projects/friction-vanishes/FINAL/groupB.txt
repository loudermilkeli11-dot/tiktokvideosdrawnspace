s05_town (17.5-22.0, tau 55.5 -> 60)  samples 8, 12 for the first 0.4 s   BEST FRAME: 1.50
  Camera on the WEST headland (-470, ground + 30 m, -1520), 50 mm -> 54 mm with a 30 m push, looking along the west arm's curved
  beach/promenade (key action y ~300-750). NOT the lighthouse headland: from there the town is 1-1.7 km away (cars < 4 px) and the
  view faces into the kit SUN (backlit). Buildings within 260 m of the lens are removed (they filled a third of the frame).
  Every object slides on bay groundH by the same law (g along the local slope - KM v|v|), integrated at load (dt 0.05); buildings
  hold (paths into a building are rejected); in water: buoyant (g x 0.3) + heavy water drag, so they stall in the shallows.
  DRAMATIZATION / deviation: integrated honestly from tau 0, the town drains before tau 50 (nothing would still be pouring at 55-60).
  So each object is jammed in a pile against the buildings until a staged release time o.tr, then slides from rest by the law.
  ~310 objects (232 cars, props, 8 boats); ~92 water entries during the shot, each with a splash scaled by entry speed.
  Beach slump: terrain vertex shader + ocean depth lowered by up to 1.6 m along the shore (JS twin for the objects); per-pixel
  waterline in the ocean shader. Brown-olive churned water within ~150 m of the shore (ocean fragment patch, sun-lit mud colour).
  Opens on the tail of s04's wipe: 900 dark drops on the lens falling out of frame by ~0.35 s (frame 0 dark, ramps to the bay).
  Weak spots: at the last frame things are still moving: grip returns ON the cut (tau 60 = film 22.0), so the "everything stopped"
  handshake state can't happen inside s05; the hard cut + hit carries it. Splashes are small at 300-900 m. Mild saw-tooth on the
  waterline at the bottom right (coarse terrain mesh vs the slump). The churn is olive-grey more than brown. Cars are boxy at this
