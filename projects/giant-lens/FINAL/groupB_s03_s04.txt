s03_tower (film 9.0–13.5): BUILT, checked on the GPU. Full size 0.3 s/frame (8 samples; 12 in the first 0.55 s of the tilt).
  - Tower = city.js building #52 (glass, x 1.5–46.5, 72.6 m tall). We see its south face (z −74.1), the sunlit one: SUN.x < 0,
    so the beam can't reach the east-street face. The camera sits on the plaza's north edge (45.2, 1.7, −46), 24 → 30 mm, yaw −30.
    The tilt continues from +35° pitch, settles near 44°, then follows the climb to about 57°.
  - Spot (spotOf(D): 0.6 → 1.9 m as the lens grows 63 → 200 m over 0.2–1.0 s) comes up the facade and dwells on one pane
    (floor 8, x 32.8). That pane reaches flow at 1.88 s (GLASS_MELT_S after arrival). It glows, sags into an orange molten tongue
    and drips. The spot then climbs to 59 m with a slight S. Panes it only crosses crack from thermal shock and burst out as shards.
    Behind it stays a black, ember-edged gash of holes plus a createBurn char line. The heat is a per-pane simulation, precomputed
    at load from the law's flux with radiative cooling.
  - Lens: 200 m, handle at upper left (ha 2.2), cone into the spot. Its shadow on the facade is drawn only on the tower face
    (an own decal; the kit decal is an unbounded plane).
  - Type zones: the spot stays at x ≈ 300–350 and y ≈ 330–520, clear of the counter column and the caption band.
  - Key frames: out/frames_s03/s03_tower_t1.50.png (dwell), t3.00 (molten tongue + shards), t4.30 (BEST: gash + tongue,
    the handshake into hit 1), full/s03_tower_t4.30.png.
  - Weak spots: the falling drips are small (2–4 px at half size; they streak once motion blur is on). The molten tongue is a
    single pane, so the "pour" is modest. Some shards read as flat teal triangles up close.

s04_bay (film 13.5–18.0): BUILT, checked on the GPU. Full size 0.4 s/frame (12 samples).
  - CAMERA DEVIATION from the brief ("50 mm from the beach"): the 632 m lens hangs 573 m up and 256 m toward +Z, so no −Z beach
    camera can show the lens and its spot together. We look UP-SUN (+Z) instead, from the water off the pier end: pier-local
    (lx 75, d 215), 12 m up, 20 mm, aimed at the pier's middle. The pier is the leading line, the town is behind it, and the lens's
    lower rim plus its handle (ha −1.45, hanging down) fill the top of the frame. Agreed with the s05 builder, whose s05 also looks
    +Z from the north headland.
  - Spot: at the cut it is already on the deck at the shore end, mid-grow (1.9 → 5.9 m). It races out along the deck TOWARD us,
    and planks ignite where it passes (flames t0 = the spot's passage time, plus a char line). It swings over two moored boats
    (B at 1.17 s, A at 2.25 s) and each catches fire. At 3.2 s it leaves the pier end onto the water: the N5 boil jet (white
    droplets, a thin vertical jet, small clumps; no veil).
  - End (3.9–4.5): crane up (≈ 9.6 m/s at the cut) and back toward −Z, tilting down onto the burning pier. Handshake: the pier
    fire is visible across the whole crane.
  - Fixes local to this shot (kit untouched):
    (a) The rig's core/halo billboards are oversized for a 5.9 m spot, and the water/deck clipped them into a hard white box.
        After rig.set I resize core = 0.75 w and halo = 1.7 w, lift them, and set depthTest = false.
    (b) The kit lens-shadow decal (depth-tested on the plane through the spot) left pale holes on the water. I hide it and draw
        createLensShadow on a fixed plane at y = 0.3 with no depth test (renderOrder 17).
    (c) The lens, cone, spray, flames and shadow are hidden from the ocean mirror.
  - Key frames: out/frames_s04/s04_bay_t0.20.png (cut: lens mid-grow, fire starting at the shore end), t2.60 (racing line + boat),
    t3.40 (BEST: whole pier ablaze, spot at the end), t4.30 (boil jet + crane), full/s04_bay_t3.40.png.
  - Weak spots: at the cut the action is small and far (the growing lens carries the first second). splash.createBoats hulls look
    blocky up close (the foreground boat). The boil jet reads as droplets more than one solid jet. The spot sits near the caption
    band's top edge (y ≈ 520–580) late in the shot, after the caption ends at tt 3.9.
