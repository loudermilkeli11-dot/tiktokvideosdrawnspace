s05_town (18.0-22.5, 632 m -> 2 km at 0.0-0.8, spot 19 m)
- DEVIATION: the camera looks UP-SUN (+Z). It is a drone 70 m over the lagoon at (-200, 70, -680), 28 mm, yaw 170 deg, pitch 2.5 deg,
  looking across the bay at the south-shore town. Why: the lens hangs 0.91·D up and 0.41·D toward +Z from its spot, so a −Z
  hillside camera can never hold the 2 km lens and its spot in one frame. From +Z the lens's lower rim plus the handle
  (ha −1.45, hanging down into the sky) fill the top third: it reads instantly as a magnifying glass. s04 (other session)
  also looks +Z, so the crane matches: s04 ends rising ~9.6 m/s, backing −Z 4.5 m/s and tilting down 6 deg/s; s05's first 0.6 s
  continues the same motion, decelerating (up 2.9 m, back 1.35 m, pitch −1.8 deg).
- Action: the lens grows over the bay (0.2 = mid-grow). The spot sweeps the town east -> west (0.5-2.6): every roof it crosses
  catches at its passage time (flames from the real bay.buildings instances), with a continuous ground fire line between them.
  The pier still burns from s04. From 2.6 the spot turns onto the bay and runs toward camera in a boiling white jet. The kit
  core/halo billboards are resized to the spot (w·0.75 / w·1.7, with a px floor), lifted, and drawn with depth test off (they
  clipped into a hard white box at 1 km).
- Best frames: out/frames_s05/s05_town_t1.50.png (lens + handle + burning town + pier) and t4.30 (burning swath + boil jet).
  Full size: out/frames_s05_full/s05_town_t2.20.png, 0.5 s/frame at 8 samples on the GPU.
