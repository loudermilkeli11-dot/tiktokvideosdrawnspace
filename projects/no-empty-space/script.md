# no-empty-space: the board

**What if atoms had no *empty space?*** · 70.0 s · 2,100 frames · 12 shots · 30 fps · Studio v2 type (LOOK.md: no bands).
Law: `src/story.js` (every size, weight, depth and time on screen is computed there). Kit: `src/kit.js` (look, the harbour
town `src/harbour.js`, the squeeze effect `src/squeeze.js`). Overlay: `overlay.js`. Score: `score.json`. Builders: `BUILDERS.md`.

**The law.** Squeeze the empty space out of an object and it packs to nuclear density, 2.3×10¹⁷ kg/m³, keeping its mass:
d = (6m / πρₙ)^⅓. A squeezed speck still weighs m, so it falls through rock as if through air (½gt², drag ignored).
**Liberty (post caption only):** each squeeze is instant (shown over 0.25–1.2 s) and its energy is ignored; the Earth's
collapse is shown over 3 s.

**The place.** *Porto*, a Mediterranean hillside harbour town (our own): pastel houses (ochre, coral, mint, sky blue)
stacked up the slope under terracotta roofs, green shutters, arches, laundry lines, bougainvillea, a church with a
tiled dome, a quay street with cafés, Vespas, a fruit cart and fishing boats, a docked ferry (`FUORI SERVIZIO`), and on
the east headland an unfinished 381 m glass-and-stone landmark tower with cranes and an `OPENING 2027` banner. A
1,500 m mountain stands behind the town. **Hero vantage:** the café terrace `BAR DEL PORTO` (`FRAME1_CAM`), flower-box
railing in the foreground, looking north over the quay and the harbour to the headland tower. Golden hour from the left.
**Hero:** a stocky man in a straw hat, lime-and-white striped shirt and red shorts, car keys in hand, seen from behind.
