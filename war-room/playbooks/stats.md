# Playbook: review the numbers (the Commander's short order is enough; follow this method)

1. **Read the data.**
   - channel/results.csv (the newest row per video wins).
   - Any new screenshots in channel/analytics/ (Read the images).
   - channel/postmortems/ and the brain's Evidence and Lessons.
2. **Compare like with like.**
   - Use seconds watched per view, the 3 s hold, shares and saves per 1,000 views, and follows per 1,000.
   - Never compare percent-watched across different lengths.
   - Compare each studio film with its closest past post, and with the niche's hit rates (saves 5–9% of likes, shares 2–7%).
3. **Find the ONE bottleneck** that, if fixed, would most raise reach: the hook (first 3 s), the middle, the loop, the packaging (cover, caption, tags, slot), or the topic. Cite the numbers behind it.
4. **Write the result.**
   - **What happened:** 3 bullets of numbers.
   - **The bottleneck:** with its evidence.
   - **The ONE change the next video makes**, and how we'll know it worked: the number to watch and the target.
   - **Missing data:** what to screenshot next time (retention curve, traffic sources, 3 s hold).
   - With fewer than 5 videos of full stats, say that conclusions are guesses.
5. **Update channel/BRAIN.md:** Evidence, Lessons and Open questions only. Keep it under ~1,000 words, and replace stale lines.
